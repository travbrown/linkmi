I built Linkmi because Linktree charges $9–24/month for features that are trivially simple to implement — custom themes, click analytics, link scheduling, social icons, and animated effects. It's a config-driven static site you can fork, edit a single JavaScript file, and deploy to Vercel in under five minutes, with no server, no database, and no subscription required. The result is a genuinely beautiful link-in-bio page with aurora background animations, particle constellation effects, and 3D card interactions — everything you'd pay for elsewhere, completely free and self-hosted.
